NerdCommand OS

Every signal becomes an accountable Command.

A task says "do something." A Command says: produce this outcome, under this authority, through this process, by this owner, before this deadline, with this evidence.

01 / SENSE

Detect

A customer, metric, system, or AI detects a signal.

02 / DECIDE

Interpret

A person or rule determines the next action.

03 / AUTHORIZE

Check

Policies, budgets, and approvals are verified.

04 / EXECUTE

Act

A human, workflow, or AI completes the work.

05 / VERIFY

Prove

Evidence is collected and outcome confirmed.

06 / LEARN

Improve

Results improve future processes.
