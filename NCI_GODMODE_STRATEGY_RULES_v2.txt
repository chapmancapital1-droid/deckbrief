NCI GODMODE v2 STRATEGY RULES
Target: MT4 / MQL4 / AI-validated NCI trading brain
Purpose: catch pips while keeping the trader alive. Survival first, profits second.

NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLE
The EA may propose trades, but the AI/dashboard may only validate or reject those proposals. AI must not invent trades from a market snapshot. If data is missing, stale, contradictory, or unclear, reject.

SYSTEM MODE
0 = AUTO: let market regime select scalp-style or swing-style thresholds.
1 = SCALP: short-term London/NY execution, stricter spread and candle math.
2 = SWING: higher-timeframe alignment, larger RR, fewer trades.

RAW NCI SCORE
Keep the original 12-voter NCI score because it is useful for continuity:
1 DMA high/low
2 Stochastic
3 DMA slope
4 RSI/AEXD divergence
5 Candle pattern
6 ATR
7 HTF trend
8 Robotrick
9 Tick-volume activity
10 RSI slope
11 MACD
12 Day-range pattern

GODMODE WEIGHTED SCORE
A trade must pass the old raw NCI score and the new weighted score. The weighted score fixes the old problem that noisy single-bar voters had the same power as higher-timeframe trend.

Weights:
H4 EMA200 hard-direction filter: +20
MTF stack M15/H1/H4: +15
HTF trend: +15
ADX regime: +10
DMA location/break: +10
MACD momentum/cross: +10
Candle math: +10
Volume/spike validation: +8
ATR and ATR/spread validation: +8
Stochastic: +5
RSI slope: +3
Day range: +6
Order block reaction: +10
VWAP direction: +5 optional, disabled by default because MT4 spot FX uses tick volume.

Default thresholds:
Scalp minimum weighted score: 85
Swing minimum weighted score: 105
Minimum weighted gap between chosen side and opposite side: 12
Raw NCI minimum score: 8 of 12 minimum for data gathering, tighten to 9-10 after forward data.

HARD REJECTION GATES
Reject if any of these are true:
- Outside allowed session.
- Weekend or Friday disabled when Friday skip is on.
- Spread is above max.
- ATR/spread ratio is weak.
- ATR is below pair-adaptive minimum.
- ADX is below chop threshold.
- H4 EMA200 direction disagrees with trade direction.
- MTF stack fails for direction.
- Candle math fails.
- Exhaustion candle blocks the trade.
- Breakout requirement fails when enabled.
- Reward/risk below mode minimum.
- Daily loss kill switch triggered.
- Weekly loss kill switch triggered.
- Max trades per day reached.
- Consecutive loss cooldown triggered.
- News blackout is active in the controller/dashboard.
- AI validation does not return approved=true from both Ollama and Claude.
- Human/dashboard approval is missing.

CANDLE MATH
A candle pattern is not valid unless the candle has measurable body and location. This fixes the old bug where a 0.1-pip body could count as a pin bar.
Required by default:
- Minimum signal body: 0.8 pips
- Minimum signal range: 2.5 pips
- Minimum body percentage of full candle range: 22%
- Buy close must be near top of candle; sell close must be near bottom.
- Reject oversized signal candles above 1.8x ATR.
- Reject opposite wick exhaustion.

VOLUME POLICY
MT4 forex volume is tick volume, not centralized real exchange volume. Use volume as an activity/liquidity filter, not as proof of institutional buying or selling.
Preferred volume confirmation:
- Volume ratio above lookback average.
- Optional z-score for spikes.
- Never approve volume-only trades.

SCALPING MODEL
Use only in liquid sessions, preferably London/NY overlap.
Requirements:
- Strong weighted score.
- Tight spread.
- ATR/spread ratio passes.
- Candle math passes.
- MACD momentum supports direction.
- Volume/activity confirms.
- RR minimum: 1.20, but prefer 1.50+.
- Small risk per trade, default 0.25% max.

SWING MODEL
Use when H4/H1 direction is clear.
Requirements:
- H4 EMA200 direction must agree.
- MTF stack must agree.
- ADX should not be chop.
- Entry should occur near level/order-block/retest rather than middle of nowhere.
- RR minimum: 1.80, prefer 2.00+.
- Fewer trades, wider stops, more patience.

PROFIT PROTECTION
Default management is staged:
Stage 1: move stop to breakeven after BE trigger.
Stage 2: after profit reaches Stage2ProfitPips, use ATR trail.
Stage 3: after profit reaches Stage3ProfitPips, use chandelier-style trail.
Never widen a stop. Never grid. Never martingale. Never average down.

ACCOUNT SURVIVAL RULES
Default max risk per trade: 0.25%.
Max daily loss: 1.0%.
Max weekly loss: 3.0%.
Max trades per day: 5.
Max consecutive losses: 3.
Max one trade per symbol/magic unless manually changed after testing.

AI VALIDATOR RULES
Ollama and Claude must return strict JSON only. They validate the EA proposal, not raw-market fantasies. Approval requires:
- Proposal action is BUY or SELL.
- Proposal is current and not expired.
- Blockers are empty.
- Symbol, timeframe, magic, SL, TP, RR, spread and risk match rules.
- The GodMode weighted score and raw NCI score meet thresholds.
- Risk and live-trading flags are correct.

TESTING RULE
The included logic harness only tests decision gates. It is not a market backtest. Before live trading, run MT4 Strategy Tester and forward demo with broker spreads, slippage, news blackout, and real execution logs.
